# Website content

The owner builds website pages and manages the facts that those pages show.

## Language

**Page**:
A visitor destination, such as Contact. Its language versions use one place in the website package's page allowance.

**Language version**:
The words and layout of one page in a chosen language. It can be edited and published separately from the other versions.
_Avoid_: A new purchased page

**Shared fact**:
One business value that applies to every language, such as an item's price or the shop's phone number.

**Entry**:
One item in a content collection, such as a stationery item or category. Translating its name does not make another item.
